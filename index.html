Research Scientist · Medical AI

I’m a Medical AI researcher based in Seoul, Republic of Korea. My work focuses on the end-to-end development of clinically validated AI models across CT, whole slide imaging (WSI), and fundus imaging, spanning detection, classification, segmentation, and prognostic modeling using real-world clinical and industry datasets.

I’m currently a Research Scientist at VUNO Inc. (military obligation) and I’m open to research collaborations.

Research

My research is grounded in multi-institutional hospital collaborations (Severance Hospital, Kyungpook National University Chilgok Hospital, Dankook University Hospital, Samsung Medical Center, and others). I develop medical AI with real-world clinical data and workflows in mind, spanning data curation, modeling, interpretability/evaluation, and clinical validation.

RWD · Collaboration

Multi-institutional Clinical Research (Hospital Data)

End-to-end, clinically oriented projects built with hospital partners using real-world data. I design studies to generalize across sites, handle heterogeneous acquisition protocols, and produce evidence aligned with clinical deployment.

Clinical AI

Clinical-Grade Imaging AI (CT · Pathology WSI · Fundus)

Imaging models for detection, classification, segmentation, and prognosis—developed under practical constraints and evaluated on clinically meaningful settings.

Robustness

Data-Centric Robust Learning

Reliability under imperfect supervision and distribution shift: noisy-label detection and dataset refinement, plus OOD screening to reduce brittle behavior in real-world deployment.

Foundation / SSL

Representation Learning & Medical Foundation Models

Self-supervised representation learning to improve data efficiency and transfer across tasks and institutions, with modality-aware inductive biases for medical images.

Evaluation

Deployment-Oriented Evaluation & Validation Protocols

Metrics and validation protocols tailored to clinical adoption: operating-point aware evaluation, CAD-specific performance reporting, and multi-center generalization assessment.
